Learning Path

From first principles
to production hardware

Follow this structured path whether you're just starting out or filling in specific gaps. Each stage builds on the last.

Stage 1 — Foundation
Electrical Fundamentals

Before touching a PCB tool, build mental models for how electricity behaves. These concepts appear in every other topic.

Voltage, Current & Power Ohm's Law Grounding Return Current Paths Pull-up & Pull-down Floating Inputs Digital Logic Levels
Stage 2 — Circuit Analysis
Analyzing Circuits on Paper

Learn to calculate voltages, currents, and component values before reaching for a simulator.

Kirchhoff's Laws Voltage Divider RC Circuits Filter Basics
Stage 3 — Passive Components
Capacitors, Inductors & Magnetics

Deep understanding of passives separates good designs from great ones. Know what every cap and ferrite is doing.

Capacitor Basics Capacitor Types Bypass Caps Decoupling Inductor Basics Ferrite Beads
Stage 4 — Power Design
Power Supply Design

Power is the first thing you design and the last thing you want to get wrong. Covers regulators, protection, and sequencing.

LDO Regulators Buck Converter Fuse Selection TVS Diodes Reverse Polarity Power Sequencing
Stage 5 — Switching & Drive
MOSFETs & Load Switching

How to switch loads safely — from small signal GPIO to high-current motor and solenoid control.

MOSFET Basics N-Channel Gate Resistors Flyback Diodes Low-Side Switching High-Side Switching
Stage 6 — Communication
Digital Communication Protocols

Hardware-level implementation of I2C, SPI, UART, CAN, and RS-485 — not just the protocol theory, but the actual circuit requirements.

I2C I2C Pull-ups SPI UART CAN Bus CAN Termination RS-485
Stage 7 — PCB Layout
PCB Design & Layout

Translate schematics into manufacturable boards. Trace widths, ground planes, component placement, and design for manufacture.

PCB Overview Trace Width Ground Pours Decoupling Placement 2-Layer Stackup 4-Layer Stackup DRC Rules
Stage 8 — Advanced Topics
Signal Integrity & EMC

For boards running at speed — or that need to pass EMC certification. Manage reflections, ringing, and radiated noise.

Ringing Controlled Impedance Differential Pairs EMI Basics Return Paths Common Mode Chokes
Stage 9 — Application
Build a Complete Project

Put it all together by working through a full reference design from schematic to assembled board.

Solenoid Controller ESP32 Minimum Board CAN Node Motor Driver Robot Control Board
