About Hardware Handbook

Hardware Handbook is a practical reference for hardware engineers, embedded developers, robotics engineers, students, and makers.

Mission

To provide clear, practical, engineering-focused explanations of electronics, PCB design, power systems, communication protocols, debugging, and hardware development.

Who Is This For?

  • Electrical & Electronics Engineering Students
  • Embedded Systems Engineers
  • PCB Designers
  • Robotics Engineers
  • Makers & Hobbyists

Topics Covered

  • Fundamentals
  • Circuit Analysis
  • Power Electronics
  • MOSFETs
  • PCB Design
  • Signal Integrity
  • EMI/EMC
  • Communication Protocols
  • Embedded Systems
  • Robotics Electronics

Roadmap

The handbook is continuously updated with new chapters, design examples, checklists, and real-world engineering lessons.
